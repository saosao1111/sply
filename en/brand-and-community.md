## Brand direction

SPLY should be described as a launch platform with standardized launch infrastructure, not as a single-token gimmick site. The strongest brand themes are:

- launch standards
- template extensibility
- coordinated market transition
- developer ecosystem

## Communication guidance

When introducing SPLY publicly, the most accurate framing is:

- a template-driven launch platform
- serving token teams, developers, and integrators
- focused on standardizing creation, fundraising, finalize, and public state

## Contact

For business, integrations, template onboarding, or media coordination, use the official Telegram or X channels as the first point of contact.
